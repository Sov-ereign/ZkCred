Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about zero-knowledge proofs on Midnight Network.

Showing 5 questions MIDNIGHT PREPROD VERIFIED
How does AegisID protect my credit score and annual income?
AegisID evaluates your financial and age credentials inside a local PLONK zero-knowledge circuit running in your browser. The circuit verifies that your score and income meet predefined thresholds, but calls disclose() exclusively on the final boolean outcome. Raw credentials are never transmitted over the wire or stored on-chain.
Why do I need the Midnight Lace Wallet?
Midnight uses shielded transactions. The Lace extension signs and submits transactions to the Midnight Preprod testnet, manages unshielded/shielded balances, and interfaces directly with the local proof server.
What are the default eligibility thresholds?
By default on Midnight Preprod, the contract requires: Minimum Age ≥ 21 years, Minimum Credit Score ≥ 700, and Minimum Annual Income ≥ $50,000 (5,000,000 cents). The contract administrator can adjust these thresholds via the updateThresholds circuit.
What is the salt nullifier and why is it used?
The salt nullifier is a domain-separated persistent hash of a 32-byte private salt. It prevents the replay of a specific salt commitment on-chain, avoiding double-verification attacks without revealing the applicant's private identity.
Can relying parties verify my eligibility without trusting the global state?
Yes. AegisID records qualifying verifications in the eligibleNullifiers on-chain set. Relying parties invoke the checkNullifierEligible(nullifier) query circuit to independently confirm whether a specific verification succeeded, unaffected by subsequent caller activity.
