Zero-Knowledge Architecture

How AegisID Works

Built on Midnight's Compact language — the privacy boundary is enforced by cryptography, not promises.

01

Private Input

Enter your credit score and income locally. This data is provided as a private witness — it never leaves your device.

02

ZK Proof Generation

The Compact circuit evaluates your eligibility inside a zero-knowledge circuit. The proof server generates a cryptographic proof locally.

03

Selective Disclosure

disclose(eligible) is called deliberately. Only the boolean result crosses the privacy boundary to the public ledger.

04

On-Chain Verification

The proof is submitted to Midnight Preprod. The blockchain confirms your eligibility. Your raw financial data stays invisible forever.

How does AegisID protect my credit score and annual income?
Privacy & ZK

Your credit score and annual income are supplied exclusively as private witness inputs inside your browser. They are processed locally inside a PLONK ZK-SNARK circuit. Only the resulting boolean result (isEligible: true/false) is disclosed on-chain via Midnight's Compact language.

Why does Lace Wallet require unlock before connecting to Midnight?
Lace Wallet

Lace Wallet manages your Midnight shielded keys securely. Before signing ZK transactions or authorizing dapp connection, the wallet extension must be unlocked so it can derive keys and interact with the Midnight network RPC API.

What are the default eligibility thresholds for verification?
Eligibility

By default, the AegisID ZkCred circuit evaluates age ≥ 21, credit score ≥ 700, and annual income ≥ $50,000. These thresholds can also be dynamically configured on-chain by contract administrators.

Was this helpful?
Where is the ZkCred Compact smart contract deployed?
Smart Contract

The contract is written in Midnight Compact and compiled to ZKIR. It is deployed on the Midnight Preprod testnet, accessible via the official indexer and proof server endpoints.

Was this helpful?
Can an observer replay my generated zero-knowledge proof?
Privacy & ZK

No. AegisID incorporates a unique 32-byte domain-separated salt and replay nullifier into every verification call. Replaying an identical proof will be rejected by the Midnight consensus engine.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

How It Works

Your sensitive data never leaves your browser. Only a cryptographic proof of eligibility is submitted on-chain.

1

Private Witness (Local Only)

Your age, credit score, and income are entered locally in your browser. They are passed as private witness inputs to the Compact ZK circuit — they are never serialized, never sent to any server, and never appear in any transaction payload.

2

PLONK ZK Circuit Evaluation

The Compact smart contract circuit evaluates age ≥ 21 && creditScore ≥ 700 && income ≥ $50k inside a PLONK zk-SNARK constraint system. The proof is generated without revealing any of the private values.

3

Selective Disclosure On-Chain

Only isEligible: true/false is disclosed to the Midnight public ledger via disclose(). Any observer can verify the result — but cannot learn your actual age, score, or income.
